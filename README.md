## About

Poke Alerts tells collectors the moment Pokémon TCG product goes back in stock. More than 2,000 monitors watch over 50 retailers worldwide, and when something lands the alert reaches Discord in seconds, with a direct link to the listing.

Over 25,000 members use it, and they have posted more than 37,000 successful checkouts in the `#buy-success` channel.

[poke-alerts.com](https://poke-alerts.com) · [Join the Discord](https://poke-alerts.com/discord)

## What you get

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Restock alerts | Over 2,000 live monitors across every major retailer, delivered to Discord in seconds. |
| Price drops and deals | Price movement and deal alerts from 50+ retailers worldwide. |
| Product intel | Verified allocations and release dates from retail partners, ahead of the drop. |
| In-store stock checker | Check local store inventory near you before making the trip. |
| Region and store filters | Only get pinged for the stores and regions you can actually buy from. |
| Weekly giveaways | Sealed SPCs and ETBs given away to members every week. |

## Getting started

1. [Join the Discord](https://poke-alerts.com/discord) and read `#start-here`.
2. Start a trial from [poke-alerts.com](https://poke-alerts.com). Premium is $5.99/month with three days free, billed through Whop, and you can cancel any time.
3. Set your region and store filters so alerts match where you shop.
